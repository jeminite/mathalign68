Your class against New York State, question by question

A working analysis, shared in case it is useful. The item data and the statewide P-values are NYSED's own, published with each year's released questions. Where a question is shown, its wording comes from the PDF's own text layer character for character and only the mathematics is reconstructed -- but reconstructed maths can be wrong, so every item links to the official page and that page is the authority. Curriculum alignment is a judgement call by one teacher rather than official guidance. Corrections and disagreement are welcome.
